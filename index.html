Dolphin Vertebra

About this specimen

A single bone from a dolphin's spine, worn smooth by sand and surf. Choose a number to explore the parts of the bone.

How to use

Touchscreen

RotateDrag one finger across the bone
ZoomPinch two fingers together or apart
Read a partTap a numbered circle, on the bone or in the panel
Back to the startTap the i button in the panel
Start / stop turningTap Spin or Pause at the top right
Back to the opening viewTap Recentre at the top

Mouse

RotateLeft-click and drag
PanRight-click and drag, or Shift + left-click and drag
ZoomScroll wheel
Read a partClick a numbered circle, on the bone or in the panel

Keyboard

Rotate←→ turn, ↑↓ tilt
Zoom+ closer, − further away
Read a part1 2 3
Back to the startI or Esc
Reset the viewR
Start / stop turningS toggles
Free lookDrag, scroll, or use the arrows at any time, even on a marker

Choosing a numbered part turns the bone to face that feature and holds it still. Pause spin keeps it still permanently, which is useful when talking a class through one feature.
